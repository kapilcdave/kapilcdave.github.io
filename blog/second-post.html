Getting Started

The events leading up to this dream were nothing special. I did almost everything I normally do except this time I was up around 1 AM studying for CS61A. I spent almost the entire day studying and I think that may have been what caused this dream.

How it Happened

My alarm originally went off at 9:15 AM and had I woken up then, there would be no way I would have had a dream. I did what I usually did and turned the alarms off and went back to sleep.

The Dream

Once I feel asleep again, I remember phasing into a dream. I can't recall the beginnings of it but I recall the excitement of a mythical adventure. Having recently watched the first episode of Steel Ball Run from Jojo's Bizarre Adventure, I have a feeling my dream was directly influenced by events from that. I have recently been obsessed with this anime and the story is ver inspiring.
